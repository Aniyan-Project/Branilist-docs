The API must not be used to copy or reconstruct the Branilist database.

- Systematically collect the entire catalog or substantial portions of it.

- Distribute datasets obtained from the API, even when the original access was free.

Caching and local storage proportionate to the application's operation are permitted, including to improve performance or provide temporary offline access. Such storage must respect the documentation, the integration's purpose, and changes to privacy or deletion.

These restrictions do not prevent a user from exporting their own data through authorized Branilist resources.
